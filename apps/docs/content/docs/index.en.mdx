**Meuproduto** helps small offices create, organize and track their documents. The idea is to replace the Word file copied and edited by hand with templates whose fields are filled in from the client record, with a history of versions and signatures.

<Callout type="warn" title="Under construction">
  Meuproduto is under development. This documentation only describes what
  already exists; areas that are not ready yet are marked as under construction.
</Callout>

## Who it is for

- **Office administrator:** manages the team, the templates and the permissions.
- **Operator or assistant:** registers clients, fills in documents and follows their progress.

## How your data is handled

- Documents and client data are treated as confidential.
- Each office only sees its own data.
- A signed document is never changed: a change creates a new version.

## How to use it

- **Search:** use the search field at the top, or the `Ctrl K` shortcut, to find a page by subject.
- **Language:** the documentation is available in Portuguese and English. Switch with the language selector in the menu.
- **Theme:** choose light, dark, or the theme of your operating system.
