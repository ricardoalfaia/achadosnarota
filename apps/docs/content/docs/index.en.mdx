**Achados na Rota** helps people on the road discover places worth the trip: restaurants, cafés, inns and experiences reviewed by an independent curation team. With the **Achados na Rota Passport**, subscribers also get benefits offered by partner establishments.

<Callout type="warn" title="Under construction">
  Achados na Rota is under development. This documentation only describes what
  already exists; areas that are not ready yet are shown as under construction.
</Callout>

## Who it is for

- **Visitor:** explores places and reads the curation reviews, no account needed.
- **Subscriber:** saves favorites and uses the passport benefits.

## How curation works

- Each place is visited by the curation team, which publishes the score, the review and the visit date.
- Establishments do not pay for scores, seals or ranking.
- Benefits are separate from the review and always show their rules before use.

## How your data is handled

- We only ask for name, e-mail and password to create an account.
- Your favorites and your passport are visible only to you.
- The app does not use your location.

- **Search:** use the search field at the top, or the `Ctrl K` shortcut, to find a page by topic.
- **Language:** the documentation is in Portuguese and English. Switch with the language selector in the menu.
- **Theme:** choose light, dark or your operating system theme.
