## How to sign in

1. Open the Meuproduto address your office received.
2. Enter your **e-mail** and **password**.
3. If you are an administrator, confirm with the code from your authenticator app.

People signing in for the first time receive an **invitation** by e-mail, with a link to create their password.

## Security

- Administrators always use a second factor in addition to the password.
- After a few wrong attempts, access is blocked for a while.
- When you change your password, your other open sessions are ended.
