## Create an account

1. Tap **Entrar** at the top of the app and then **Criar conta grátis**.
2. Enter your **name**, your **e-mail** and a **password** with at least 10 characters.
3. Accept the terms of use and the privacy policy.

## Sign in

1. Tap **Entrar** and type your **e-mail** and **password**.
2. You go back to the screen you were on.

Forgot your password? Use **Esqueceu a senha?** on the sign-in screen: the link to create a new one arrives by e-mail and is valid for 1 hour.

## Security

- After a few wrong attempts, access is blocked for a while.
- When you change your password, your other open sessions are ended.
