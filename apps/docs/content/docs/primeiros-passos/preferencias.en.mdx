In the **user menu**, opened from your avatar in the top right corner, you choose how the system looks for you.

## Theme

| Option | What it does                                |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------- |
| Light  | Light background.                           |
| Dark   | Dark background.                            |
| Auto   | Follows the theme of your operating system. |

## Language

The system is available in **Português (Brasil)** and **English**. The language applies to the menus and screens of the system.

<Callout title="Where preferences are kept">
  Preferences are kept in the browser. On another browser or computer, choose
  again.
</Callout>
